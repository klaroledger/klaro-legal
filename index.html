Personal finance

Your money, quietly under control.

Klaro Ledger is a private, offline-first money tracker for Android. Log spending in seconds, set budgets and goals, and see your whole financial picture — all on your own device, with no account required to start.

What Klaro does

Track in seconds
Log expenses & income fast, with categories, receipts and a calendar view.
Budgets & goals
Budgets that roll over month to month, and savings goals that grow with you.
Whole net worth
Accounts, cash and investments in one view, with spending insights.
Multi-currency
Track across currencies at historical exchange rates.
Shared spaces
Share a budget with a partner or household — end-to-end encrypted.
Widgets & backup
Home-screen widgets, plus encrypted backup to your own Google Drive.

Private by design

  • Your financial data lives on your device, encrypted at rest.
  • No account needed to start — no email, no sign-up wall.
  • No ads, and no third-party tracking of what you do in the app.
  • Backup and shared spaces are end-to-end encrypted: backups go to your private Google Drive folder, and our shared-spaces relay only ever holds ciphertext — never your numbers.
